Hello, I'm Maneet.

I'm a sophomore at Yale (Branford College, Class of 2029) studying Statistics & Data Science and Physics & Philosophy. I work on questions at the intersection of artificial intelligence, governance, and ethics: how generative models encode bias, how public institutions should divide work between people and automated systems, and what human oversight actually costs.

Currently

  • Visiting researcher at the Centre for Digital Governance, Hertie School (Berlin), in Prof. Joanna Bryson's group, leading a study of human oversight burdens and AI task allocation in public-sector administrative processes.
  • Undergraduate Learning Assistant for S&DS 2380, Probability and Bayesian Statistics.
  • Analyst at the Yale Undergraduate Consulting Group; Senator and Dining Policy Chair on the Yale College Council; member of the Yale Debate Association and the Yale Foreign Policy Initiative; fellow with Yale AI Alignment.

Previously

  • First-authored a study of emotion bias in generative image models with Dr. Cody Buntain at the University of Maryland (arXiv:2411.05985).
  • Built a hybrid NLP pipeline for tracking U.S. force-posture commitments in defense policy documents with the German Council on Foreign Relations and the Yale Foreign Policy Initiative.
  • Developed LockdownGPT, an adversarial-attack demonstration for large language models, at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory.
  • Delegate to the 2025 U.S. Senate Youth Program; Eagle Scout and Maryland Eagle Scout of the Year (2025).
