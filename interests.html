Interests

The parts of my life that don't fit neatly on a résumé.

Debate

I have debated competitively since high school, first in Lincoln–Douglas, where I finished in the national top 20 in 2023 and won the Maryland state championship in 2024, and now in the British Parliamentary and APDA formats with the Yale Debate Association. I still think it is the best training available for holding two incompatible positions in your head at once and taking both seriously.

Scouting

I earned Eagle Scout with Troop 540 in Howard County, Maryland, where I served as Senior Patrol Leader for a troop of more than seventy scouts. My Eagle project assembled tree-planting kits for the Community Ecology Institute. In 2025 the American Legion named me Maryland Eagle Scout of the Year.

Reading

I read widely across classical history, political philosophy, political economy, and literary fiction. Lately that has meant a good deal of Max Weber, whom I have been studying in a seminar, alongside the histories that first drew me to statecraft as a subject. My favorite film is 12 Angry Men, which is also, I would argue, the best film ever made about deliberation.

Training

I lift, and I take the programming side of hypertrophy training more seriously than is probably reasonable. I have been vegetarian my whole life, which makes hitting protein targets an ongoing engineering problem.

Languages & Home

I grew up in Columbia, Maryland, in an Indian-American family, and I speak Gujarati at home. My Spanish is intermediate and improving. Berlin, where I spent the summer of 2026 doing research, is the city I most want to return to.
