  • The Strategic Posture Commission and the Two-Adversary Problem
    Memo · Yale Foreign Policy Initiative · December 2025 · PDF

    A brief on the Strategic Posture Commission's report. The Commission argues that the United States is entering a harder deterrence era in which it must be prepared to deter, and if necessary respond to, near-simultaneous challenges from two nuclear adversaries, and that plans designed for a one-major-war paradigm will be stressed in the 2027 to 2035 window. The memo summarizes the report's core findings and recommendations for nuclear strategy, force structure, and the nuclear enterprise.
